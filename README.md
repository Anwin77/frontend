Xylo is a minimilastic social media page catered towards student communities to share their ideas and collaborate on mutliple exciting innovations and ideas.
 Here on Xylo, Students can freely express themsevles with other like minded individuals on topics of common interest.
This simple social media platform also serves as a way for students to find new people and make aquaintances and also hopefully find some flourishing friendships!!!❤❤


Xylo is Super Easy to use:
- Register to your Account
